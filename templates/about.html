Where we started

StudyPlan.com began as a class project in MIS 3010. The idea took shape in the 12:05 p.m. lecture on March 30, 2026, when Izzy and her classmates imagined a single place for college students to track projects, exams, homework, deadlines, and assignments without losing them across apps and sticky notes.

That lecture became our origin story: students building for students—priorities that actually match how coursework feels, a calendar that respects real schedules, and tools like grade estimates and progress views so you always know where you stand.

Today, StudyPlan.com is our answer to that question: What if your whole semester lived in one calm, organized workspace?
