How Cole's Kicks started

One lecture, one idea, one marketplace.

Origin

March 30, 2026 — MIS3010 at 2:15 p.m.

Cole's Kicks was born in the 2:15 p.m. lecture of MIS3010 on Monday, March 30, 2026. What began as a course group project quickly took shape as a full buyer-and-seller sneaker resale concept.

Cole, serving as CEO, rallied classmates around a simple mission: make it easy to list authentic-style sneakers, discover inventory with real e-commerce patterns, and prove out purchase flows, order tracking, and trust-building pages—all in one Flask-powered site.

Classmates split responsibilities across merchandising mock data, UX for buyers vs. sellers, and storytelling on the About, Help, and Careers pages. Every button and filter is there to showcase how a resale platform could work when the semester ends and the idea keeps running.

Today Cole's Kicks is a demo marketplace: mock payments, real structure, and a nod to the lecture where it all started.
