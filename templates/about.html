About StumpSolutions

Born in a classroom. Built for backyards.

Our origin story

StumpSolutions was founded at 9:55 a.m. during the MIS 3010 lecture on March 30, 2026, by Aaron and his classmates. What started as a course project quickly grew into a fictional-but-fierce brand promise: show up on time, grind deep, and make the whole experience unexpectedly delightful.

In the real world, you’d swap this paragraph for your actual company history, licenses, and insurance details.

Mission

Erase stumps with precision and personality—so homeowners can reclaim their yards without dreading contractor roulette.

Values

Safety first, transparent quotes, respect for property, and a little sparkle—because yard work doesn’t have to feel gray.

How we work

  1. You share stump count, rough size, and access—we respond with a range or schedule a walkthrough.
  2. We mark the work area, confirm utilities awareness, and protect turf with boards and mats.
  3. Our crew grinds to the agreed depth, contains flying chips, and shapes the hole for mulch or backfill.
  4. Final walkthrough: you approve, we sweep, you celebrate a stump-free life.
